What does View settings do?
Light & Soft shadows
Adjust brightness and switch soft shadows on or off.
Floor material
Choose the original floor, concrete or oak parquet.
Floor surface controls
Metalness changes the metallic character; Roughness changes gloss (lower is glossier); Surface detail changes the strength of the fine texture.
Floor reflections
Switch reflections on, then use Reflection controls to adjust their strength and blur. Switching them off can improve performance.
Wall color
Change the booth walls; artworks and trim keep their colors.
Detail
Choose Balanced or High rendering resolution. High may be more demanding on your device.
Reset buttons
Restore the defaults for that group. Choosing another floor also resets its surface controls.

Reopen anytime with Info.
