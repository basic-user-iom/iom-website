International Space Station model
=================================

Source: NASA/JSC/Integrated Graphics, Operations, and Analysis Laboratory (IGOAL)
Source page: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/international-space-station-iss-d-igoal/
Published by NASA: 20 May 2026

This repository contains a web-optimized derivative of NASA's official GLB.
The geometry, material grouping, and textures are retained; mesh and texture
transport were reduced for interactive browser rendering. Exact source and
output checksums and the complete transformation record are in manifest.json.

NASA's media guidelines state that files used in the rendition of 3D models
are generally not subject to copyright in the United States and may be used
for informational purposes. NASA should be acknowledged as the source. This
use does not imply NASA review or endorsement.

Guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
